Before you start

Are these the real tests?

No. MindMetric is independent and is not affiliated with any test publisher or employer. Our questions are original and written to match the published format and timing of each test.

How much time do I need?

Most online assessments arrive with a deadline of about a week. A focused hour a day is enough to learn the formats and run a couple of timed simulations. A single module drill takes about five minutes.

What's free?

Every module has free practice questions you can try without an account. Create a free account to take a timed full test and save your progress. No card needed.

Does the membership renew?

No. A membership gives you one month of full access to every test and then ends on its own. There's nothing to cancel.
