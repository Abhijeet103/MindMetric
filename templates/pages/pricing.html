Common questions

Does the membership renew automatically?

No. You pay once and get one month of access. When it ends, your account goes back to the free plan and keeps your history. There's nothing to cancel.

Does one membership cover every test?

Yes. It covers every test and module on MindMetric, so you can prepare for several employers' assessments at once.

Are these the real tests?

No. MindMetric is independent and not affiliated with any test publisher or employer. Our questions are original and follow the published format and timing of each test.
