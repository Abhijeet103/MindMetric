# UK-focused hiring preparation

Company lookup now submits company, role and location through its existing single action. The dropdown uses network-derived country, with a browser-timezone hint when network location is unavailable. Explicit or previous search choices take precedence. Unknown locations stay at Any location. It offers UK cities and other countries, plus remote/any-location choices. Location is already part of the database cache identity; UK, city and overseas guides remain separate. Research instructions prioritise early screening assessments, preserve evidence-based stage order, and require geographic limitations to be stated. No paid model call is needed for practice generation.

New original practice tracks:

- `situational_judgement`: six scenario families (privacy, workload, accuracy, customer service, disagreement, accessibility), with explicit decision principles and explanations. Higher difficulty adds authority and recordkeeping constraints. Scoring follows the supplied rubric, not an inferred personality profile.
- `work_simulation`: four linked decisions per scenario, sharing an inbox, policy and operational data. Tasks cover priority, shortfall arithmetic, pack-size purchase estimates and selecting an evidence-based email response. This first release uses selected answers, not free-text assessment.
- `verbal_reasoning`: original passages with True, False and Cannot say decisions, including necessary/sufficient conditions, sample limitations and causal uncertainty.
- `aptitude`: arithmetic (percentages, ratios, averages, rates, profit, series), verbal logic (ordering, inclusion chains, letter coding), and visual patterns (existing original visual engine under MindMetric subject practice).

Every new module supports Foundation / Intermediate / Advanced in untimed practice. The selector preserves the existing practice flow; timed sessions continue to use administrator-configured difficulty. Question content is generated locally, with no LLM cost. Scores are practice results, not calibrated employer pass marks. No examination branding or copied question bank is used.

The release migration enables tracks for the platform tenant only. Institution allocation remains controlled through tenant administration. Generic sourced assessment names can match the new tracks; bespoke employer assessments continue to be saved as suggestions rather than being claimed as exact replicas.

Validation covers question/answer validity, answer secrecy in timed mode, 100 distinct items per new section and level, shared simulation exhibits and independently computed cost answers, route rendering, location validation/caching, research recommendation matching, and tenant administration.
